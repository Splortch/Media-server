# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and patient. People with different experience levels are welcome. Critique code and proposals, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and sexualized conduct are not acceptable.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and project-related community spaces. Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through their GitHub profile's published contact channel. Do not post personal information in public reports. A formal team reporting mailbox has not yet been established.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or remove repeat/severe offenders. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and open to review by the other maintainer. Conflicted maintainers should recuse themselves.
